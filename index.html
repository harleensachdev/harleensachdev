Soundscapes of Distress: Few-Shot Learning for Cross-Species Avian Alarm Call Detection

Neural Computing and Applications · April 2026 · University of Nottingham collaboration

Designed a hybrid few-shot learning framework for cross-species avian alarm call detection across 889 hours of field soundscapes. Demonstrates the viability of data-efficient ML approaches in noisy, real-world audio environments — directly applicable to production systems with limited labelled data.
